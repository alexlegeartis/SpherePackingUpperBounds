# Exact transfer of a scalar cyclic polynomial from41D to42D

The same scalar angular polynomial Q and radial profile h satisfy the same cyclic inequality on triangle side/angle variables regardless of ambient dimension. If Q_new=lambda Q_old with lambda>0, the cyclic sum scales positively, so its sign is unchanged. This does not automatically transfer positive definiteness: the target42D normalized Gegenbauer expansion must be recomputed and checked nonnegative. The complete verifier reconstructs that expansion exactly, checks constant coefficient1, all coefficient signs, Q_new=lambda Q_old coefficientwise, B_new=lambda B_old, and identical h. It then uses the42D kernel, class inequalities and normalization. The source41D complete centered-gradient cyclic gate is replayed from scratch. Its previously passed primary gate is not counted as a second fresh gate here. There is no dimension transfer of the density bound itself.
