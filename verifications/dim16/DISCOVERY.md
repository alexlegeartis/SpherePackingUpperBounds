# Discovery provenance

The separately distributed **16D discovery and exact-export companion**
contains the successful search chain in three portable subfolders:
`profile_search`, `count_search`, and `radial_search`. Its README gives
the frozen inputs, parameters, numerical solver settings, optional search
commands, and precise reproduction limits. No absent historical research
directory is required for the released proof or exact export.

The companion's `export_exact.py` reconstructs the final mathematical
inputs from the saved successful search outputs and verifies their SHA256
identity against this proof package. The radial part is reconstructed from
the normalized Laguerre coefficients and the one exact correction; it is
not merely copied from the final proof file. No fresh optimizer replay is
claimed by the exporter or by packaging.

The proof package itself remains fully self-contained and requires none
of the discovery programs. The original 107MB source file and unused SOS
blocks are omitted; the complete exact a/B/F factor subset, original source
URL and source hash are present. Both new spherical inequalities are
verified directly instead of assuming a widened contact certificate.

Unsuccessful degree/scale screens, uneconomic guard-only repairs and later
factor-row pilots are summarized in the companion without importing their
unusable candidates. The original radial positive pair point is retained
as a proof negative control. None of the rejected expressions is a bound.
