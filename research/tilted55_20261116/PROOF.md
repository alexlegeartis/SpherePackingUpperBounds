# A certified tilted 55D lattice and a correction to the previous audit

The new lattice has minimum **exactly 10** and center density
**91,274.808704090862…**. It improves the valid, period-repaired
order-three lamination by **0.900919%**, with squared density ratio
**225/221**. Its complete rational Gram is [gram55.json](gram55.json).

**No world-record claim is made.** This density is below the
116,783.149646… printed in Leterrier's thesis. The displayed construction
for that larger claim has an explicit norm-6 period vector, however, and
does not establish the claimed minimum 10. We retain the printed claim
as disputed rather than silently calling a lower comparator the old SOTA.

The previous repository claim that this 55D Gram was locally optimal
at minimum 10 was wrong and is withdrawn. The 54D, 56D, 57D and 58D
certificates do not use that 55D lattice.

## Exact verification

```

```

Requires NumPy and python-flint. The auditor repeats the source-to-Gram
reconstruction of Leterrier54 from 124 polynomial points, checks the
non-narrow point's pairings and order, builds the 55D Gram, and checks all
layer inequalities and density comparisons using rational arithmetic.
No new closest-vector enumeration or completeness assumption about a
finite shortest-vector list is needed.

The inherited published inputs are the narrow lattice's minimum 10 and
the lower bound 28/3 in its two nontrivial non-narrow cosets. They follow
from the height bounds in the [2022 paper](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40993-022-00321-0)
and [2024 thesis](https://infoscience.epfl.ch/record/307330/files/EPFL_TH10417.pdf),
Theorem 3.2.7 and the height calculation preceding Proposition 3.2.22.
The faulty 55D lamination proposition is not an input to our minimum proof.

## External-source correction

Let L be the rank-54 narrow lattice. We use its published minimum 10 and
determinant 3^25. The non-narrow point Q has

```
3Q in L,    Q not in L,    ||Q||²=28/3,
min ||Q+L||² = min ||−Q+L||² = 28/3.
```

The thesis, Proposition 3.2.22, pp.155–156, uses the lattice generated by
`(L,0)` and `(Q,h)` with `h²=2/3`, and computes a density using minimum 10.
But this lattice contains

```
3(Q,h) − (3Q,0) = (0,3h),    ||(0,3h)||² = 6.
```

In our archived Gram the coefficient row is `(-q3,3)`, where `q3=3Q`
in the 54D basis. Its full integer coordinates and exact norm are in
[results.json](results.json). The original lattice actually has minimum
6: layer zero inherits minimum 10, layers ±1 have norm at least 10,
layers ±2 have norm at least 12, and all other nonzero layers have
vertical squared norm at least 6. Thus the obstruction is an explicit
short vector, not uncertainty about an uncomputed minimum.

The general lamination argument in Proposition 1.2.9, pp.24–25, omits
the case where different layers have the same horizontal coset. Their
index difference can be a nonzero multiple of the number of cosets.
For the displayed period, the additional necessary condition is
`N² h² >= minimum(L)`. Here `N=3`, and `9*(2/3)=6<10`.

Increasing `h²` to `10/9` repairs that construction. The inherited coset
bound suffices to certify minimum 10 for the repaired lattice, and its
center density is

```
delta54 = (5/2)^27 / 3^(25/2),
delta55,repaired = (3/2) delta54 = 90,459.838739557352… .
```

This is a **derived repair of an external construction**, not the density
printed by its author. The printed value remains recorded separately.
We have not disproved the existence of some other packing achieving that
printed density; we have rejected its displayed construction and our
earlier use of it as a certified guarantee.

## The tilted construction

Choose any supplied integer minimum vector v of L, so `||v||²=10`. Set

```
alpha = 2/45,
s = alpha v,
h² = 442/405 = 10/9 − ||s||²,
L55 = < (L,0), (Q+s,h) >.
```

The Gram is rational, with positive Schur complement `h²`. Its
determinant is `3^25*(442/405)`. There is no requirement that a sphere
packing lattice have integral Gram entries.

For a coset W of L with minimum at least D, and `0<=t<=1`, both w and
w+v lie in W. The exact identity

```
||w+t v||²
 = (1−t)||w||² + t||w+v||² − t(1−t)||v||²
 >= D − 10t(1−t)
```

therefore bounds the entire shifted coset, rather than a supplied subset.
Every lattice vector lies in an integer layer k. Negation reduces the
minimum checks to nonnegative k:

* `k=0`: the parent minimum is 10.
* `k=1`: using D=28/3, the squared norm is at least
  `D−10 alpha(1−alpha)+h²=10`.
* `k=2`: since 2Q is congruent to −Q modulo L, the squared norm is at least
  `D−10(2alpha)(1−2alpha)+4h²=116/9>10`.
* `k=3`: the horizontal coset is `3s+L`. Since
  `||3s|| <= sqrt(10)/2`, the triangle inequality and the parent minimum
  show that zero is a nearest parent point. Its minimum squared norm is
  `9||s||²`, and the total is `9(||s||²+h²)=10`.
* `k>=4`: the vertical contribution alone is at least `16h²>10`.

The coefficient row `(-q3,3)` now has squared norm exactly 10, proving
attainment as well as the lower bound.

## Density and comparison scope

The squared center density is

```
delta55² = (5/2)^55 / (3^25*(442/405)).
delta55² / delta55,repaired² = 225/221 > 1.
```

It is only `135/221` times the *printed* 55D squared density. Consequently
this package supplies a new valid packing and a strict improvement over
the repaired comparison, **not an improvement over that printed number**.
The range table marks this distinction and does not register 55D as a
world-record dimension. The other unresolved dimensions in 49–96 remain
54, 56, 63, 64, 72, 80 and 92; 55D has a new construction but no new
record claim.

## Separate parity-check experiments in 54D, 56D and 80D

We also tested index-128 sublattices using seven general binary checks.
Removing all norm-10 vectors in 54D/56D, or all norm-14 vectors in 80D,
would let evenness raise the inherited minimum to 12 or 16, respectively.
The corresponding potential gains outweigh the index 128 cost. The
search used only the supplied minimum-vector orbits; it made no
completeness assumption for a positive certificate.

In the capped 20-restart search, the best remaining known-ray counts
were 26 in 54D and 8 in 80D. Three 56D candidates removed all 2,436 known
rays, but reduction found a norm-10 vector in every candidate. Their
seven-check matrices and exact counterexamples are retained in
[parity_screen.json](parity_screen.json); the main auditor checks all
three counterexamples and rank 7 of the checks. These are rejected
candidates, not new lower bounds or nonexistence theorems.
