## Proof of the weaker 86D extension

The bound remains `delta86=2^46/(27*sqrt(3))`, or
`1,504,719,014,574.4526…`. Its historical **116.264339%** gain over
Chen's theorem guarantee is correct but uses a weaker comparison than
the BCS88 section above; it is not a record improvement.

Use the 80D parent's short-vector counts and quadratic-space populations
already proved in [COUNTING_PROOF.md](COUNTING_PROOF.md). For a singular
binary class, excluding norms 8,12,16 guarantees half-coset depth at least 5.
The bad-class upper bound and common population lower bound are

```
B0 = 344425245676960140000,
D  = 2^79 - 2^39 - 1.
```

For a totally singular binary subspace of rank r, the expectation of the
number of bad nonzero classes is at most `(2^r-1)*B0/D`. It is strictly
less than 1 for every `r<=10`; thus a good subspace exists. Both quadratic
types in dimension 80 have Witt index at least 39, so these subspace
models exist. The same finite-orthogonal-group averaging proof applies.

For `r=6`, take the vertical lattice `M=sqrt(3/2)*E6`, of minimum 3 and
Gram determinant `(3/2)^6*3 = 2187/64`. Nonzero binary layer classes have
horizontal norm at least 5 and vertical norm at least 3; all-even nonzero
layers lie in 2M and have norm at least 12. The parent attains 8.
Block triangularity gives full Gram determinant `2187/64`, hence
`delta86^2 = 2^92/3^7`.

The script also checks the analogous rank-7/8/9/10 constructions using
E7, E8, E8⊕A1, E8⊕A2. **All four 87–90D bounds are below the classical
MW92 section controls in the table.** They are retained as weaker
existence constructions, not reported as improvements. Rank 11 fails
this particular union bound; no nonexistence conclusion follows.

The new root Grams are checked to be positive definite, integral and even,
with diagonal 2. Therefore their minimum is exactly 2 without a short-vector
enumeration. Their determinants and every averaging inequality are exact.

## Classical section controls from MW92

For a d-dimensional lattice of packing radius r and covolume v, choose a
shortest nonzero vector u in the dual. It is primitive, and the section
perpendicular to u has covolume `v*||u||` and minimum at least `4r^2`.
Hermite's inequality on the dual gives

```
||u||^2 <= gamma_d * v^(-2/d),
delta_(d-1) >= delta_d^((d-1)/d) / sqrt(gamma_d).
```

Blichfeldt supplies `gamma_d <= (2/pi)*Gamma(d/2+2)^(2/d)`.
Source: [Blichfeldt, Math. Ann. 101 (1929), 605–608](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01454863).
Starting at the **published** rank-92 MW theorem (`min=16`,
`det=47^14`) gives the section controls down through 84D.

`verify_to96.py` proves rational upper bounds for each Hermite constant
by raising to the d-th power. For even d the gamma function is a factorial;
for odd d its square is a rational times pi. A rational enclosure of pi
comes from Machin's arctangent identity and alternating-series remainder
bounds. Floating calculations only propose an upper bound and an integer
squared-density floor; exact integer/rational inequalities certify both.
Each recursive density floor is checked on both sides after raising to
the d-th power. No floating value is accepted as a proof inequality.

MW92 products in 93–96D have density squares
`delta92^2/(2^r*det(R_r))`, for `R_r=A1,A2,A3,D4`. These are ordinary
classical products. The new torus constructions exceed all four.

## Exact algebra for the recent 88D parent

The catalogue data [ideal115L0](https://www.math.rwth-aachen.de/homes/Gabriele.Nebe/LATTICES/dim88a.html)
was downloaded, reduced, and sampled using its two listed isometries.
Their identities were checked exactly. A 72-ray sample already contained
a primitive normal `K=[[8,-4],[-4,8]]`. With labels `{0,e1,e2}` its
diameter is `1/6`. A primitive norm-10 normal with labels `{0,1}` gives
diameter `1/10` in 87D. Thus, **if the parent's minimum is 8**,

```
delta86^2 = (3/16)*(47/24)^86,
delta87^2 = (2/5)*(79/40)^87.
```

The archived source, integer normals and labels are included. The auditor
checks positive definiteness, determinant 1, both normal Grams, saturation,
complete period determinants, and every cluster distance. Its historical standalone output
reports that this checker does not verify the parent minimum. The separate
[full source replay](../priority_resolution_20261115/REPLAY88.md) now discharges
that computational gap and checks equality with this exact parent Gram.
An LLL basis alone would not establish minimum 8.

With the completed source replay, the parent-minimum computation has
been reproduced. Nevertheless both antipodes are below the classical
BCS88 section controls and cannot support record claims. Credit for the
recent parent and its minimum proof belongs to Sakamoto, not this repo.
