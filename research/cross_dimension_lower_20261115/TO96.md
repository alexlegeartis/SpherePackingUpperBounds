# Lower-bound research with a ceiling of 96 dimensions

The research range is **1–96D inclusive**, following the user's limit.
The new calculations here target 86–96D. No construction above 96D is
used as an input to these calculations.

**New result:** the compatible-coset counting argument above the published
Bachoc–Nebe L80 proves an **86D** minimum-8 lattice of center density

```
delta86 = 2^46/(27*sqrt(3)) = 1,504,719,014,574.4526… .
```

It exceeds the guarantee reconstructed from
[Chen (2013), Theorem 2.4 and §3](https://doi.org/10.5802/jtnb.825)
by **116.264339%**. This comparison is with a published guarantee, not
with a proven current worldwide record. Explicit horizontal representatives
are not supplied by the counting proof.

For 86D, Chen's displayed logarithm is `39.3225`; the exact theorem
parameters give `39.3398354535…`. As in the 84D/85D audit, the comparison
uses the larger theorem-derived guarantee.

**Additional conditional results:** actual primitive normals in the
catalogue's claimed Sakamoto 88D lattice give explicit antipodes in 86D
and 87D. Their center densities are **1,540,370,290,434.3653…** and
**4,551,964,265,436.0571…**, conditional on the preprint's minimum-8
theorem. The minimum claim is marked as claimed by the authors in the
catalogue and has not been independently replayed here. These conditional
packings therefore do not supersede the unconditional proofs in the table.

## Proof of the 86D extension

Use the 80D parent's short-vector counts and quadratic-space populations
already proved in [COUNTING_PROOF.md](COUNTING_PROOF.md). For a singular
binary class, excluding norms 8,12,16 guarantees half-coset depth at least 5.
The bad-class upper bound and common population lower bound are

```
B0 = 344425245676960140000,
D  = 2^79 - 2^39 - 1.
```

For a totally singular binary subspace of rank r, the expectation of the
number of bad nonzero classes is at most `(2^r-1)*B0/D`. It is strictly
less than 1 for every `r<=10`; thus a good subspace exists. Both quadratic
types in dimension 80 have Witt index at least 39, so these subspace
models exist. The same finite-orthogonal-group averaging proof applies.

For `r=6`, take the vertical lattice `M=sqrt(3/2)*E6`, of minimum 3 and
Gram determinant `(3/2)^6*3 = 2187/64`. Nonzero binary layer classes have
horizontal norm at least 5 and vertical norm at least 3; all-even nonzero
layers lie in 2M and have norm at least 12. The parent attains 8.
Block triangularity gives full Gram determinant `2187/64`, hence
`delta86^2 = 2^92/3^7`.

The script also checks the analogous rank-7/8/9/10 constructions using
E7, E8, E8⊕A1, E8⊕A2. **All four 87–90D bounds are below the classical
MW92 section controls in the table.** They are retained as weaker
existence constructions, not reported as improvements. Rank 11 fails
this particular union bound; no nonexistence conclusion follows.

The new root Grams are checked to be positive definite, integral and even,
with diagonal 2. Therefore their minimum is exactly 2 without a short-vector
enumeration. Their determinants and every averaging inequality are exact.

## Classical section controls from MW92

For a d-dimensional lattice of packing radius r and covolume v, choose a
shortest nonzero vector u in the dual. It is primitive, and the section
perpendicular to u has covolume `v*||u||` and minimum at least `4r^2`.
Hermite's inequality on the dual gives

```
||u||^2 <= gamma_d * v^(-2/d),
delta_(d-1) >= delta_d^((d-1)/d) / sqrt(gamma_d).
```

Blichfeldt supplies `gamma_d <= (2/pi)*Gamma(d/2+2)^(2/d)`.
Starting at the **published** rank-92 MW theorem (`min=16`,
`det=47^14`) gives the section controls down through 84D.

`verify_to96.py` proves rational upper bounds for each Hermite constant
by raising to the d-th power. For even d the gamma function is a factorial;
for odd d its square is a rational times pi. A rational enclosure of pi
comes from Machin's arctangent identity and alternating-series remainder
bounds. Floating calculations only propose an upper bound and an integer
squared-density floor; exact integer/rational inequalities certify both.
Each recursive density floor is checked on both sides after raising to
the d-th power. No floating value is accepted as a proof inequality.

MW92 products in 93–96D have density squares
`delta92^2/(2^r*det(R_r))`, for `R_r=A1,A2,A3,D4`. These are ordinary
classical products. They are useful explicit targets for a new search.

## Exact algebra for the claimed 88D parent

The catalogue data [ideal115L0](https://www.math.rwth-aachen.de/homes/Gabriele.Nebe/LATTICES/dim88a.html)
was downloaded, reduced, and sampled using its two listed isometries.
Their identities were checked exactly. A 72-ray sample already contained
a primitive normal `K=[[8,-4],[-4,8]]`. With labels `{0,e1,e2}` its
diameter is `1/6`. A primitive norm-10 normal with labels `{0,1}` gives
diameter `1/10` in 87D. Thus, **if the parent's minimum is 8**,

```
delta86^2 = (3/16)*(47/24)^86,
delta87^2 = (2/5)*(79/40)^87.
```

The archived source, integer normals and labels are included. The auditor
checks positive definiteness, determinant 1, both normal Grams, saturation,
complete period determinants, and every cluster distance. It explicitly
reports that the parent's minimum claim was **not independently verified**.
An LLL basis containing norm-8 vectors does not establish minimum 8.

This is a concrete reason to withhold a worldwide claim for the new
unconditional 86D bound: a slightly higher 86D guarantee follows if this
new source theorem is accepted. Both constructions are from this experiment;
the source paper is credited with the parent, not these numerical consequences.

## 96D claims and the research range

The [November 2026 four-Leech construction page](https://srfp311t1.com/rank96_four_leech)
explicitly states that a complete minimum-10 certificate for its candidate
is unavailable. A separate master-manifest search result asserts a stronger
claim, but that does not override the construction page's stated gap.
No minimum-10 96D theorem from this source is used as a proven benchmark.

Within the user's 1–96D range, the priorities are now:

| Range | Research decision |
| --- | --- |
| 1–48 | Use current published benchmarks; retain the explicit 36D improvement over KP36. Recent 39D/43D antipode work must be included in new searches there. |
| 49–64 | Continue 57–59D full-rank dual-pool work; keep the stronger named classical comparator in 59D. |
| 65–72 | Retain the explicit 65D construction; seek the missing embedding for the conditional 66D normal. |
| 73–85 | Find explicit representatives for the counting constructions; retain the external 84D/85D comparison audit. |
| **86–87** | New unconditional 86D proof plus explicit source-qualified 88D-parent antipodes; resolve the new parent's minimum status before a record claim. |
| **88–92** | Use the classical MW92 section controls and distinguish the recent 88D claim from independently replayed data. |
| **93–96** | Search for compatible deep shifts or richer finite clusters that beat MW92 product controls; recover the quantitative BCS96 comparison before a global claim. |

This establishes research targets and additional lower bounds, not a
complete catalogue of worldwide records through dimension 96.
