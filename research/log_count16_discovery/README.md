# Portable 16D discovery and exact-export companion

This compact companion records the successful discovery chain for the
separately verified strict upper bound 0.024920988. It is not itself a proof.
The 107MB original source archive member, unused SOS blocks, large redundant
LP matrices, and rejected later factor-row candidates are not included.

## Deterministic exact export

Run `python export_exact.py`. It writes five mathematical inputs to a
temporary directory and checks their SHA256 values against the frozen proof:
the profile, Q, original exact factor subset, reweighted count candidate,
and repaired radial candidate. The radial export reconstructs all normalized
Laguerre polynomials from the saved binary64 search coefficients, applies
the exact normalization and one 1/1000000 correction, and reproduces the
proof input byte for byte. No optimizer is called. `export_check.json`
records the completed export check; it does not imply fresh optimization.

Requirements for export: Python 3 and python-flint. Requirements for optional
search replay: NumPy, SciPy and python-flint. Set BLAS/OMP thread counts to 1.

## Included successful search chain

- `profile_search/` is the complete small fixed-Q log-hinge experiment,
  including its source Q, fixed old controls, source pair-dual pricing,
  coefficient/knots settings and saved radial coordinate grid.
  `python log_hinge.py` in a temporary copy reruns its one profile fit
  and, if sampled separation passes, one degree 63 radial LP.
- `count_search/` contains the source's exact factor subset, its portable
  evaluator, whole-harmonic block reweighting program, final history,
  log and reference row pool. Starting without the program's writable
  checkpoint, `python reweight.py --cap .5003 --rounds 12 --resume
  --margin .000001` follows the recorded deterministic row-generation
  setup. The last history row is the candidate exported for exact proof.
  `reference_final_rows.npz` is an audit reference, not an automatically
  loaded warm start. The original factor source and its hash are recorded
  inside factors.json.
- `radial_search/` contains the conditional radial search, exact Laguerre
  helper, frozen base grids and critical-root diagnostics. `python
  conditional.py` in a temporary copy performs the original conditional
  U=7803 solve and at most one exact-root-guided refit. Its large LP
  matrices are regenerated by the script rather than archived twice.
  The saved final refit is then exported with the deterministic correction.
  The proof uses the independently certified integer count 7802 without
  changing the pair function or the count multiplier.

All optional searches should run in disposable copies: their scripts write
new outputs. No fresh optimizer replay was performed during packaging, and
floating optimizer solutions are not guaranteed to be bitwise identical
across versions. The exact exporter and full proof replays, rather than
optimizer status, establish the identity and validity of the released inputs.

## Sensitivity decisions and excluded work

Conditional cost pricing showed that cap 0.5003 required a count below
about 7827.338 to reach the comparator with the chosen profile. A simple
added angular guard cost about 7883.040 and was rejected as uneconomic.
Whole-block reweighting reduced the real count objective to 7802.215167...
and then passed exact direct verification. The narrower cap 0.5001 could
not help even under the hypothetical count 7333.

The initial radial search output had forbidden sign islands. After one
root-cut refit it passed both rays but failed a tiny pair inequality.
The proof package preserves that exact positive point and the single
deterministic repair. Later richer factor-row pilots were rejected at
exact cyclic counterexamples and do not belong to the successful chain.
Their lower sampled objectives are not proof inputs or density records.
