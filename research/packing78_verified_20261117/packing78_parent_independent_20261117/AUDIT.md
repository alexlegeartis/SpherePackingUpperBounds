## Mathematical minimum proof and source

The external theorem is Shioda's height-pairing formula, Theorem 8.6, equations (8.11)–(8.14), in *On the Mordell–Weil lattices*:
https://rikkyo.repo.nii.ac.jp/record/10027/files/AA00610867_39-02_05.pdf
The theorem gives the height in terms of section intersections, the arithmetic genus, and local corrections from reducible fibers. This is a published mathematical input, not a numerical assumption. The primary source's theorem and formulas were checked directly through its searchable text on 17 November 2026.

Here the minimal Weierstrass surface is y²=x³+1+t^42 in characteristic 41. Each of the 42 simple roots of 1+t^42 gives an irreducible type-II fiber: the discriminant has order 2 and the constant coefficient has order 1. The total space is smooth at its cusp because the derivative with respect to t is nonzero. At infinity put s=1/t, X=s^14 x, Y=s^21 y. The equation becomes Y²=X³+1+s^42, whose fiber at s=0 is smooth. The Weierstrass fundamental line bundle has degree 7 (equivalently the minimal discriminant has degree 84), so χ=7. There are no reducible-fiber corrections.

Consequently every nonzero section P satisfies h(P)=14+2(P·O)≥14. Intersections of distinct curves are nonnegative. The supplied polynomial sections have no intersection with O, including in the infinity chart, and therefore have height exactly 14. For distinct, nonopposite supplied sections, the finite intersection multiplicity is the degree of gcd(x_P−x_Q,y_P−y_Q). The infinity contribution is the lesser of the valuations of s^14(x_P−x_Q)(1/s) and s^21(y_P−y_Q)(1/s). These follow by restricting the ideal of one section to the other in the smooth surface. Hence the reconstructed off-diagonal height is 7 minus these two contributions; opposite sections have height pairing −14.

Finally, YG Yᵀ and integral generation identify the abstract rank-80 integral lattice with the subgroup generated by these sections under the height pairing. Any integral relation having zero coordinate vector has height zero; conversely positive definiteness detects all nonzero coordinate vectors. The height formula excludes nonidentity torsion. Thus every nonzero lattice vector has norm at least 14, and the supplied sections attain 14. **It is unnecessary to claim that these sections generate the entire Mordell–Weil group.** Generation of the explicitly defined rank-80 lattice is what has been proved and what the packing requires.
