# Independent exact theta and discriminant count for Ne64

This note independently verifies the counting input used for the
65-dimensional three-layer construction. The inherited lattice fact is
that Ne64 is an even 3-modular lattice of dimension64 and minimum12. Its
published Gram matrix, determinant and minimum appear at

<https://www.math.rwth-aachen.de/homes/Gabriele.Nebe/LATTICES/CQ64b.html>.

## Why the theta expansion is determined

Use q=exp(2 pi i tau), so the coefficient of q^j counts vectors of norm2j.
Let

```
a = Theta(A2) = 1 + 6q + ...,
d = (eta(tau) eta(3 tau))^6 = q + ... .
```

The first is a modular form of weight1 on Gamma0(3), with quadratic
character chi(-3); the second is a cusp form of weight6 with trivial
character. Under the determinant-normalized Fricke involution W3 their
eigenvalues are respectively -i and -1. Therefore all six forms

```
f_j = a^(32-6j) d^j,  j=0,...,5
```

have weight32, trivial character, and Fricke eigenvalue+1. Their leading
orders are0,...,5, with leading coefficient1.

Ne64's theta function has the same transformation properties. Indeed its
level is3, its determinant3^32 is a square, and the isometry
sqrt(3)L* congruent to L gives the Fricke eigenvalue+1. Its coefficients at
q^1,...,q^5 vanish because its minimum is12.

There is thus a unique linear combination of the f_j matching the theta
function through q^5. The difference would vanish to order at least6 at
infinity. Fricke invariance forces it to vanish to order at least6 at the
other cusp0 as well. The valence bound for a nonzero holomorphic modular
form of weight32 on Gamma0(3), whose index is4, is

```
sum of cusp orders <= 32*4/12 = 32/3 < 12.
```

The difference must therefore be zero. This proves the expansion without
requiring an uninspected assertion that a particular ring presentation
exhausts all modular forms.

The resulting coefficients are

```
(c_0,...,c_5) = (1,-192,10944,-185088,536256,-483840).
```

`[q^n]a=6 sum_{d|n} chi_3(d)` and expands the eta product directly. Relevant

## Exact finite quadratic form

The primary Gram matrix G has `H=3G^{-1}` integral and even on the diagonal.
The discriminant group D=L*/L has dimension32 over F3. In dual basis
coordinates its nondegenerate quadratic form is

```
Q(x+L) = 3(x,x)/2 mod3.
```

The checker selects32 independent rows of H modulo3. Their corresponding
dual basis vectors give a quadratic matrix `H_selected/2 mod3`, whose
determinant is1. Since the dimension is32, this is the split type. The
standard finite-field quadratic counts give

```
#{Q=0} = 3^31 + 2*3^15,
#{Q=1} = #{Q=2} = 3^31 - 3^15.
```

An isotropic class has even norm residue. Below norm8, its only possible
nonzero dual norms are4 and6. The total number of vectors on these shells
is

```
138458880 + 37527682805760 = 37527821264640.
```

There are617673424981760 nonzero isotropic classes. Therefore at least
580145603717120 of them have minimum at least8. Multiple vectors in the
same class only strengthen this inequality.

For either choice h in such a class, -h has the same minimum. Since the
class has order3, the lattice generated by L x {0} and (h,2) has minimum
at least12: steps not divisible by3 have horizontal norm at least8 and
vertical norm at least4; nonzero steps divisible by3 have vertical norm
at least36. Its covolume is `2*3^16`, giving centre density

```
delta_65 = (sqrt(3)/2)*3^16.
```

The argument proves existence without identifying a particular deep
discriminant class. It does not establish the historical or current
record status of the resulting65-dimensional packing.

## The higher nonisotropic target remains open here

For the norm residue2/3, the possible dual norms below26/3 are14/3 and20/3.
Their total count is983547074776320, greater than the number of classes
`3^31-3^15`. Hence the first-moment count does not prove a class of minimum
26/3. The tempting division by3 needs a separate proof of within-class
multiplicity; the exact automorphism audit in
`NE64_DISCRIMINANT_ACTION.md` rules out the proposed global symmetry
argument inside the published subgroup.
