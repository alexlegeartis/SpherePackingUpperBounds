# Exact local E8 decoding ingredients

E8=D8 union((1/2,...,1/2)+D8). For either branch, independent nearest integer rounding followed, if necessary, by the least-cost parity correction gives its exact nearest point. Comparing both branches gives exact E8 nearest distance. All arithmetic is integer at denominator16.

For target a/16 with computed nearest point v at squared distance at most1, any E8 point y at squared distance at most5/2 satisfies ||y-v||<sqrt(8). E8 is even, so y-v has norm at most6. The frozen complete shell0,2,4,6 has1,240,2160,6720 vectors, respectively, generated by all integer or half-integer coordinates with norm≤6 and even coordinate sum. Thus all local points within threshold640/256 can be recovered by translating this9121-vector shell by v. Carry labels are exact classes in E8/L1, computed through the complementary polarization basis.

This establishes only local finite search coverage, not any80D coset depth. A code/neighbor global join is still required.
