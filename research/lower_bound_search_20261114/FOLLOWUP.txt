1. ARBITRARY COMMON NONLINEAR TERNARY FIBERS ARE TOO SMALL

This strengthens the rank-three CSP obstruction substantially. Let C be the
archived GF(4) [20,13,6] code, or any shortening at one, two or three positions.
In the fixed mixed-hexagonal quotient, use every outer word and any common
set T contained in F3^n. No linearity is assumed for T. This first result uses
packing radius sqrt(2), as in the original minimum-eight proposal. It does not
exclude changing the target minimum to nine, ten, eleven or twelve.

At squared minimum eight, T must avoid differences of ternary weight one,
and differences with nonzero support equal to a weight-six/seven support of C.
These conditions are also sufficient. At radius sqrt(2) the center density is

                  4^k * |T| / 3^(3*n/2).

Put nonnegative edge weights 120, 5 and 1 on these three forbidden classes.
For a ternary character with nonzero-coordinate support J, the eigenvalue is

  120*(2*n-3*|J|)
    + 5*sum[(-1)^|S intersect J| * 2^(6-|S intersect J|), S a bad 6-support]
    +   sum[(-1)^|S intersect J| * 2^(7-|S intersect J|), S a bad 7-support].

A tensor transform with local matrix [[1,2],[1,-1]] enumerates every eigenvalue
over 2^n support patterns. Each has multiplicity 2^|J|. If d is the weighted
degree and lambda the least eigenvalue, the weighted Hoffman bound gives

                    |T| <= 3^n * (-lambda)/(d-lambda).

All arithmetic in verification is integer or rational. The choice of weights
was suggested by a small floating LP; the certificate needs neither that LP
nor an optimization solver. Spectral moments and direct character sums are
checked independently.

For the archived length-17 shortening, d=210288 and lambda=-1872. Thus

        |T| <= 387420489/340, hence |T| <= 1139472,

whereas beating the pinned 34D record requires at least 3109875 points in T.
The unweighted bound in results/nonlinear_common_fiber_bounds.json leaves
headroom here; the weighted certificate supersedes that interpretation.

The entire parent and shortening family is excluded:

  dimension   outer codes covered   worst upper density / pinned record
      34             1140                 0.5033217559026669
      36              190                 0.4257614197898427
      38               20                 0.4434363949500344
      40                1                 0.1908564966289285

Verification streams all 4^13 words to recover all short supports, verifies
the supplied coordinate permutations on every support, and computes the
generated group's order 120. Eighteen orbit representatives cover all 1351
codes. The shortening constraint rank is checked separately for every member;
the permutations need not be automorphisms of the full code.

This does not exclude changing the outer code, selecting an arbitrary subset
of outer words together with a different fiber, word-dependent arbitrary
fibers, or changing the geometric metric.

Reproduce, requiring only NumPy:

    python verify_common_fibers.py --output work/common_fibers.json

results/common_fibers_replay.log. Verification passed.

2. WORD-DEPENDENT FIBERS WITH UP TO FOUR SELECTED TRITS ALSO FAIL

Fix the archived [17,10,6] code. For any selected set I of k<=4 trit positions,
let T0 set those trits to zero and impose parity zero on the remaining trits.
For every outer word independently, allow any union of cosets of T0, including
the empty union. Thus expurgation, different labels and different parity phases
are all allowed.

Every dangerous outer difference has weight six or seven and at least two
nonzero positions outside I. Two signed nonzero trits can attain every sum in
F3. Consequently a dangerous cross-word difference exists exactly when its
selected nonzero pattern matches the outer support on I, independently of the
remaining parity phase. At most one phase can be used for a fixed outer word
and selected tuple, because differing phases have a norm-four difference.
Conversely, choosing global trit parity zero makes all labels within one outer
word compatible. This gives an exact reduction to a Cayley conflict graph on
F2^20 x F3^k, allowing arbitrary independent sets.

One state has center density 1/3^(19/2+k). For each selected coordinate, a
nonzero outer symbol contributes Fourier factor 2 for the zero ternary
character and -1 for either nonzero character; a zero outer symbol contributes
1. Walsh spectra can therefore be updated by four shifted array lookups,
without constructing a graph with up to 84934656 vertices.

Every choice was checked: 17 singles, 136 pairs, 680 triples and 2380 quadruples.
The worst exact density ratios to the pinned 34D record are:

       k=1: 0.738724038604...
       k=2: 0.812658898166...
       k=3: 0.893125195168...
       k=4: 0.981575156280...

The closest case selects positions [1,5,9,15], zero-based. Its degree is 11844,
least eigenvalue -904, and independent-set bound 19195232256/3187. Beating the
record requires at least 6136034 states. Thus even this case fails.

This excludes only the stated fiber kernels over this fixed parent. For five
selected positions a weight-six difference can leave only one nonzero trit
outside the selected set. The reduction changes there. Applying this script's
formula unchanged for k>=5 would be invalid.

Reproduce, requiring only NumPy (the full check takes several minutes):

    python verify_selected_fibers.py --max-selected 4 \
        --output work/selected_fibers_up_to_four.json

results/selected_fibers_up_to_four_replay.log. All 3213 checks passed.

3. THE CYCLIC-CODE 34D SEED IS LOCALLY OPTIMAL AMONG LATTICES

The even cyclic GF(4) [17,8,8] code used by the earlier coset search gives a
34D mixed-hexagonal lattice with squared minimum eight and center density

              4^8 / 3^(19/2) = 1.9223302972926544...

It needs a 15.59% increase to beat the pinned 34D record. The code construction
proves its minimum without floating enumeration. Its explicit 134028 contacts
can also be generated algebraically: 1530 weight-eight outer words times 86
parity-zero sign patterns, plus 2448 zero-outer two-coordinate contacts.

For their integer coefficient matrix V and Gram Q, exact arithmetic gives

                      (V^T V) Q = 31536 I.

This is positive eutaxy. A 595-row contact witness has rank 595 over the prime
field of order 1000003, proving perfection over the rationals. Together these
properties certify a strict local density maximum among lattices by Voronoi's
criterion. A small lattice deformation cannot supply the required gain.
Periodic perturbations and other seeds are outside this conclusion.

The verifier checks the basis index, code membership, minimum, determinant,
contact generation, eutaxy identity and modular rank. No SciPy, floating-point
enumerator or lattice-reduction package is needed:

    python verify_kp34.py --output work/kp34_exact.json

Saved certificate and replay: results/kp34_exact.json and
results/kp34_exact_replay.log. Verification passed.

4. ADDITIONAL EXPLORATORY GEOMETRY — NO MINIMUM CERTIFICATES

Seven primitive sections of the certified 36D lattice were tested: three in
35D and four in 34D. Best tested densities were 2.47025577 and 1.33409059,
respectively. Each metric LP returned zero objective on all inherited contacts.
These LP outcomes remain numerical; they are not rational eutaxy certificates.
The 37D section of the newer 38D P48p complement also stalled below its record.

The alternative asymmetric CT/Turyn seed, catalogue CTturyn1, admits a
three-scale vertex (7/8,1,15/8) at squared minimum eight, with conditional center
density about 0.682956. Its 12276 returned norm-eight contacts have a ten-
dimensional exact contact-preserving kernel. Sampling vertices subject to
explicit vectors through norm ten raised the conditional density only to
about 1.194413, still far below the 36D record. BKZ found no rejecting basis
vector for the selected vertices; that is not a minimum proof.

The catalogue Beis17 34D seed leaves six exact contact-preserving directions.
Explicit vectors through norm eight bound a six-dimensional polytope with
684 numerical vertices. The best tested conditional center density was about
0.312474, far below 2.222035. This was not promoted to an exact family bound.

Numerical seeds, directions and historical logs are kept under experiments/
and results/. The replay tool can regenerate shells and metric LP screens.
Numerical contact preservation, BKZ output and floating enumeration completion
must not be called global minimum certificates or record packings.

RESEARCH IMPLICATION

The archived minimum-eight rank-three common-fiber proposal has no nonlinear
common-fiber escape at radius sqrt(2), including every one-/two-/three-coordinate
shortening of its parent. A word-dependent construction must leave the specified kernels
with at most four selected trits, change its outer code, or change its metric.
The existing cyclic-code 34D lattice also has an exact local optimum barrier.
These are concrete filters for new searches, not a proposed new density bound.
A different minimum normalization is a separate direction; see NORMALIZATION12.txt.

The registry and the certified 36D construction were not modified.
