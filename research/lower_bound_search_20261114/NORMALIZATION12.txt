A DIFFERENT CONSTRUCTION TARGET: CHANGE THE MINIMUM TO TWELVE

This is a conditional target and an implemented search, not a new density
record. It is outside the minimum-eight obstructions in FOLLOWUP.txt.

Keep the same mixed hexagonal quotient, with period squared minimum twelve.
Use a quaternary [n,k,6] code C and a ternary linear self-orthogonal code T of
dimension t. Self-orthogonality makes every ternary word's weight divisible by
three. Since a^2-a*b+b^2 == (a+b)^2 modulo three, all squared norms of the
resulting lattice are divisible by three as well.

For a ternary difference with support J and an outer difference with support S,
the exact least squared norm is

                    3*|S| + 4*|J| - 6*|S intersect J|.

The outer minimum six and self-orthogonality rule out norm three. A norm-six
violation occurs exactly when a weight-six ternary support is also an outer
weight-six support. Norm-nine violations occur exactly when a weight-six
ternary support is contained in an outer weight-seven support, or a weight-nine
ternary support equals an outer weight-nine support. These conditions are
necessary and sufficient for minimum twelve; the period attains twelve.
Thus candidate certification is finite and exact, without numerical SVP.

If these violations are absent, the center density is

                     delta12 = 2^(2*k-n) * 3^(t-n/2).

Concrete targets using the archived higher-rate quaternary family:

   packing dimension    n     k     t     conditional center density
          34           17    10     8             8/sqrt(3)
          36           18    11     8            16/3
          38           19    12     9            32/sqrt(3)
          40           20    13    10            64

Each exceeds its pinned record. The 36D target has exact density ratio
1058841/1048576 to the certified construction, a 0.9789467% conditional gain.
None of these four targets has been constructed here.

FIRST TEST: PRODUCTS OF TERNARY TETRACODES

The ternary [4,2,3] tetracode has four possible nonzero supports, each a triple
inside a four-coordinate block. For two such blocks, every union of two triples
must avoid outer six-supports and the six-subsets of outer seven-supports.
This supplies a necessary pair compatibility graph on four-coordinate blocks.

For the original C17, C18 and C20 instances, the graphs have respectively 315,
347 and 515 edges. Exhaustive clique search finds neither four compatible
blocks in C17/C18 nor five in C20. The same necessary test rejects every one-,
two- and three-coordinate shortening of C20, using 18 support-symmetry orbits
covering 1351 parent/shortened codes. In 38D it already prevents the four
tetracodes needed for a product of four tetracodes and one length-three
repetition code. No weight-nine information is needed for these rejections.

Reproduce (NumPy and a C++17 compiler):

    python verify_tetra12.py --output work/tetra12.json

The replay includes positive and negative controls. Saved outputs:
results/tetra12.json and results/tetra12_replay.log.

SECOND TEST: GENERAL SELF-ORTHOGONAL FIBERS

The search in experiments/selforth12_search.cpp starts from four tetracodes,
then reflects their span in random weight-four ambient vectors over F3.
If v has four nonzero entries, v*v=1, and

                         x -> x + (x*v)*v

is an orthogonal reflection in characteristic three. It preserves dimension
and self-orthogonality while allowing fibers that are not coordinate block
products. Simulated annealing minimizes ten times the number of bad norm-six
trit words plus the number of bad norm-nine trit words. One representative
per antipodal pair is used during the search. The best generators are saved.

Two million proposed reflection moves were tested separately for the archived
34D and 36D codes. The best returned fibers have no norm-six violation, but
still have 404 and 262 bad norm-nine trit words, respectively. Their attained
minimum is nine, not twelve. They do not beat the pinned records.

Replay a search:

    OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 python experiments/selforth12.py --dimension 34
    OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 python experiments/selforth12.py --dimension 36

Independently audit the saved best generators without rerunning annealing:

    python experiments/selforth12.py --dimension 34 \
        --audit-best results/selforth12_17_best.txt --output-prefix work/audit34
    python experiments/selforth12.py --dimension 36 \
        --audit-best results/selforth12_18_best.txt --output-prefix work/audit36

The audit re-enumerates the full outer and ternary codes, checks rank and
self-orthogonality, counts actual violations, computes the attained minimum
and compares squared densities exactly. All four conditional densities above
remain research targets. The heuristic's failure is not an impossibility
proof for general self-orthogonal fibers.
