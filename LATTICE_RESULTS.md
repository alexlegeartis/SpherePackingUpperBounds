## Scope and sources

The14D–22D and25D–35D named lattice benchmarks retain the user-supplied Cohn snapshot and attribution to[Zubrilina(2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35830).23D retains its preceding unrestricted repository comparison.36D–48D use the preceding lattice-only certificates pinned at[20b638f5d5b0](https://github.com/alexlegeartis/SpherePackings/tree/20b638f5d5b0bf00a2a4ce8d70c4e062517a502e); those packages are preserved. Detailed prior source/history notes remain available in the[pinned16November documentation](https://github.com/alexlegeartis/SpherePackings/blob/20b638f5d5b0bf00a2a4ce8d70c4e062517a502e/LATTICE_RESULTS.md).

The new scalar rank-two method and its exact collinear transfer are proved in[verifications/lattice/rank_two_20261117/PROOF.md](verifications/lattice/rank_two_20261117/PROOF.md).36D also has a directly reoptimized Fourier/profile certificate. Public mathematical inputs, shared arithmetic backends and independent-audit scope are stated explicitly. Later matrix-valued candidates are not included. No upper result establishes a lower construction, optimality or a kissing number.

## Reproduce

Install the selected package requirements. Run `python verifications/lattice/replay.py --dimension 40` for one result or `python verifications/lattice/replay.py` for the entire lattice registry. The new36–48 proof chain can also be run with `python verifications/lattice/rank_two_20261117/run_all.py`. Do not use `python -O`. Exact hashes are checked and generated outputs go to temporary storage. The full suite may take substantial time.
