## Scope and sources


The new scalar rank-two method and its exact collinear transfer are proved in[verifications/lattice/rank_two_20261117/PROOF.md](verifications/lattice/rank_two_20261117/PROOF.md).36D also has a directly reoptimized Fourier/profile certificate. Public mathematical inputs, shared arithmetic backends and independent-audit scope are stated explicitly. The subsequent independently checked 44D/45D compact harmonic-matrix results are included in[their complete replay package](verifications/lattice/compact_matrix44_45_20261117/). No upper result establishes a lower construction, optimality or a kissing number.

## Reproduce

Install the selected package requirements. Run `python verifications/lattice/replay.py --dimension 40` for one result or `python verifications/lattice/replay.py` for the entire lattice registry. The new36–48 proof chain can also be run with `python verifications/lattice/rank_two_20261117/run_all.py`. Do not use `python -O`. Exact hashes are checked and generated outputs go to temporary storage. The full suite may take substantial time.
